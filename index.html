About

Sparring Coach 1.6.0. An offline opening and sparring coach.

© 2026 SpotVibe LLC. All rights reserved. The app’s own code is proprietary to SpotVibe LLC and is not open source.

Style-mimic bots play from books built from the public-domain Lichess openings dataset (CC0), then Stockfish at a similar strength. Eval numbers stay hidden during the game. No account needed.

Attacking ideas and the thinking routine are inspired by GM Igor Smirnov’s public teaching (Remote Chess Academy). Not affiliated with or endorsed by him or RCA.
